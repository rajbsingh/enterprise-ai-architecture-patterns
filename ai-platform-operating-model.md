# AI Platform Operating Model

An AI platform operating model defines how an organization manages AI adoption across people, process, technology, governance, and delivery.

Enterprise AI success requires more than models and tools. It requires an operating model.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of an AI platform operating model is to help organizations move from isolated AI experiments to repeatable, governed, and scalable AI delivery.

---

## Core Operating Model Areas

### 1. AI Strategy

Organizations should define:

- Why AI is being adopted
- Which business problems matter most
- Which use cases should be prioritized
- What success looks like
- How AI aligns with business goals

### 2. AI Governance

Governance ensures AI is used safely and responsibly.

It should include:

- AI policies
- Risk classification
- Data privacy controls
- Model evaluation
- Human review
- Auditability
- Vendor assessment
- Regulatory readiness

### 3. AI Platform Architecture

The platform should support reusable AI capabilities.

Examples:

- Model access layer
- Prompt orchestration
- RAG services
- Agentic workflow layer
- Monitoring and logging
- Security controls
- Evaluation framework
- API integration

### 4. Data and Knowledge Management

AI systems need trusted data and knowledge.

This includes:

- Data quality
- Document governance
- Knowledge base management
- Metadata
- Access control
- Source freshness
- Data lineage

### 5. Delivery Model

AI use cases should be delivered through a structured process.

Example stages:

- Idea intake
- Use case assessment
- Risk review
- Prototype
- MVP
- Pilot
- Production
- Monitoring

### 6. Roles and Responsibilities

Important roles may include:

- Business sponsor
- Product owner
- AI architect
- Data engineer
- Security lead
- Legal/compliance reviewer
- Model risk owner
- AI governance lead
- Change management lead
